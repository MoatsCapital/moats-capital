About

Founded and operated by Jan Moats, Moats Capital is the Moats family's private investment portfolio, focused on long-term value creation across real estate, small businesses, and select holdings.

Moats Capital seeks durable assets, businesses, and properties with strong fundamentals and lasting competitive advantages for long-term growth.

Moats Capital is not open to outside investors and is not a financial services company. It exists to steward and grow the Moats family's capital.
